How to practice
  1. Full Band Watch and listen first so you know how the whole song goes.
  2. Guitar Only Learn your notes — hear exactly what the guitar part sounds like.
  3. You Play! The band plays without guitar — you're the guitarist. Play along!
  4. Start slow: set speed to 75%, loop measures 9–14 (the high chords), and turn on the count-in. When it feels easy, go to 90%, then 100%.
